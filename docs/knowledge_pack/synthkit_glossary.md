# synthkit glossary of terms

Definitions of synthkit's vocabulary, for resolving terms in
any conversation about the project.

A spec or recipe is the fingerprinted JSON contract describing
a dataset; same fingerprint means identical data forever. A
blueprint is the per-record planned truth that exists before
any artifact. The ledger is the signed record of every
deliberate corruption. A note column is a free-text table
column whose planted elements feed the outcome; elements are
weighted signal phrases, distractors are traps (denials guarded
by an excludes rule, or stable historical findings), fillers
are neutral sentences. The ceiling is the exact best achievable
score, known from planted probabilities. A campaign or ladder
is one exam compiled at three difficulty tiers; bars are the
declared pass marks (auroc, gap_max, recall, trap_max,
fix_rate, and so on); a verdict is PASS, FAIL, or INCONCLUSIVE
computed on confidence intervals, with INCONCLUSIVE carrying a
required-sample-size prescription. A showdown reports ceiling,
baseline, and vendor per tier with a selectable baseline. The
autosolver is the built-in tabular logistic baseline;
autosolver_hybrid adds label-mined note features; both publish
their real named coefficients for transparency. The vendor
chair or witness stand is the tested seat — any function of
training rows, training labels, and test rows returning
scores, including LLMs, whose unusable replies are counted as
malformed. Semantic lint generates a probe sample and measures
the recipe's promises. The bench is the five-station GUI; the
atlas is the generated drill-down system map with its
plain-English narrative layer. The openai backend is the
OpenAI-COMPATIBLE protocol door to local servers like
llama.cpp and LM Studio — a message format, not the company.

## Phase 2 and narrative terms

The profile is the parameters measured from a real extract —
distributions, weights, missingness, contamination and joint
structure — with every figure covering at least k patients. The
conditional dependency network (condnet) models the joint
distribution as conditional tables over binned columns, capturing
nonlinear and interaction structure; amplify is the dial that
strengthens, weakens or deletes any discovered relationship. The
privacy unit is the patient: k counts distinct people, not rows.
Effective sample size is the patient count for between-person
questions and the visit count for within-person ones. The fidelity
scorecard grades marginals, dependence shape and interactions; the
privacy scorecard grades exact matches and nearest-neighbour
distances. The power curve states how many patients each kind of
structure needs. The transcriber renders structured facts into
messy prose under a corruption taxonomy; the truth ledger records
what each sentence asserts. A scope trap is a negation sitting
beside a finding it does not govern ("no improvement in X"). The
note vendor chair seats a language model under a
present/current/certain contract, blind to the traps, with
unusable replies counted separately from wrong ones.
