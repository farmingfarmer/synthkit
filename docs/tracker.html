C. Open research — the genuine unknowns

These move dates. None is assembly work.

QuestionStatusWhat is known
The close gap OPEN 81.9% against an 87.9% bar on the real extract. Drift concentrated on cross-set-column token pairs.
Ring-adjacency ceiling PARKED near-identical variant columns (the pressure family); published surfaces 0/5. Affects both engines.
Attribution drift OPEN span_days driver agreement 40% against a 0.80 band. Characterized on the rules engine; the neural engine has no trim machinery, so that mechanism does not explain it here.
Three-way structure lost even with ample data OPEN on a fixture, two-way XOR survives every seed while the 3-way mostly dies. Not a sample-size problem — so more patients may not fix combination rules.
Per-patient distributions too narrow OPEN three quantities, one signature: every spread short, every low bound too high, every high bound too low. Suspect the mixture over patient centres under-dispersing. Test: compare generated centre spread against training centres directly.
An output-surface attack that survives width OPEN the nearest-neighbour adversary reads 0.500 even on a verbatim republish at 77 columns — a control that cannot catch a copy. The reconstruction adversary carries the real signal.

F. What the measures can and cannot see

Worth keeping visible, because a limitation that is not written down gets read as a result.

MeasureBlind to
Interaction surfaces (0/N in the gate) Not applicable to the neural engine, which publishes no surfaces. 0/N looks identical to total failure.
three_way Was numeric-only and rejected binary indicators — fixed today. Still bounded at three columns; nothing above that is measured anywhere.
Rank correlation A U-shape reads near zero on both tables. Curve tracking covers it.
Per-column checks Cannot see a broken relationship. A table with no structure between its columns passes all of them.
Joint AUC Saturates at width; the k rule guarantees separability on its own. Per-column AUC is the actionable number.
